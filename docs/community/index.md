## Why Community Matters

Good educational content improves through diverse perspectives and shared feedback. Whether you're new to FableFlow or already creating multi-sensory narratives, this community helps you go further.

This is a space for:

- **First-time authors** to find guidance
- **Experienced creators** to share techniques
- **Educators** to collaborate on curriculum-aligned content
- **Parents** to find stories that spark family conversations
- **Developers** to shape the platform

## Ways to Participate

### 📖 Share Your Stories
Showcase what you've created and get feedback.

### ❓ Help Others
Answer questions and welcome newcomers.

### 📚 Improve Documentation
Clarify guides, add examples, or translate resources.

### 🎨 Contribute Resources
Create templates, style guides, or best-practice examples.

### 🐛 Report & Fix Issues
Identify bugs or contribute code improvements.

[Learn more about contributing →](../contributing/guidelines/)

## Community Guidelines

We aim for a welcoming, inclusive space. Please review our [community guidelines](guidelines/).

**Core Principles:**

- **Respect and kindness** in all interactions
- **Constructive feedback** that helps others grow
- **Inclusive language** that welcomes diverse perspectives
- **Focus on learning** rather than criticism
- **Celebrate curiosity**
