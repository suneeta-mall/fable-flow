# Community Guidelines 🤝

Welcome to the FableFlow community. Please follow these guidelines so everyone has a positive experience.

## 🌟 Our Values

### Curiosity First
- Ask questions and explore ideas
- Share discoveries and insights
- Treat "not knowing yet" as where growth begins

### Inclusive Creativity
- Welcome storytellers of all backgrounds and skill levels
- Respect diverse viewpoints and creative approaches
- Support each other's creative journeys

### Constructive Collaboration
- Give specific, helpful feedback
- Share knowledge and resources
- Lift others up

### Educational Excellence
- Prioritize accuracy in educational content
- Cite sources and give credit
- Aim for age-appropriate, engaging content

## 💬 Community Spaces

### GitHub Discussions

Our primary community hub:

- **Show & Tell**: Share your FableFlow creations
- **Help & Support**: Get help with technical questions
- **Feature Requests**: Suggest improvements
- **General Chat**: Connect with members

### Blog Comments
On our Curiosity Chronicles:

- Share related experiences and insights
- Ask follow-up questions
- Suggest topics for future posts

### Story Feedback
When reviewing community stories:

- Note what works well first
- Offer specific, actionable suggestions
- Consider the target audience and educational goals

## 📝 Content Guidelines

### Story Submissions
**Acceptable Content:**

- Original stories created with FableFlow
- Educational and entertaining narratives
- Age-appropriate content for the specified audience
- Stories that inspire curiosity and learning

**Please Avoid:**

- Copyrighted material without proper attribution
- Content that promotes harmful stereotypes
- Inappropriate language or themes
- Purely promotional content

### Educational Standards
- Verify scientific accuracy
- Use credible sources
- Adapt complexity to the target age group
- Include learning objectives when relevant

## 🚀 Getting Started in the Community

### New Members
1. **Introduce yourself** in GitHub Discussions
2. **Explore existing stories** to understand our style and quality
3. **Start small** with the beginner tutorials
4. **Ask questions**

### Sharing Your Work
1. **Test thoroughly** before sharing
2. **Provide context** - your story's purpose and audience
3. **Include educational notes** if applicable
4. **Be open to feedback**

### Contributing Code
1. Review our [technical documentation](../getting-started/installation.md)
2. Check existing issues and feature requests
3. Follow our coding standards and testing requirements
4. Submit pull requests with clear descriptions

## 🛡️ Community Standards

### Respectful Communication
- Use welcoming, inclusive language
- Disagree respectfully and constructively
- No personal attacks or discriminatory language
- Stay on-topic

### Intellectual Property
- Respect copyrights and attribution requirements
- Share only content you have rights to distribute
- Credit original creators and sources

### Privacy & Safety
- Protect personal information (yours and others')
- Don't share contact information publicly
- Report concerning behavior to moderators
- Keep children's safety paramount in educational content

## 🎯 Quality Standards

### Story Quality

- **Clear narrative structure** with beginning, middle, and end
- **Engaging characters** for the target audience
- **Educational value** that enhances learning
- **Technical quality** in multimedia elements

### Educational Value

- Align with educational standards when applicable
- Include discussion questions or extension activities
- Provide accurate, age-appropriate information
- Inspire further exploration

## 📢 Communication Channels

### For Help & Support

- **Technical Issues**: GitHub Issues
- **General Questions**: GitHub Discussions
- **Story Feedback**: Community showcase threads
- **Private Concerns**: Direct email to maintainers

### For Contributions

- **Story Submissions**: Community showcase
- **Bug Reports**: GitHub Issues
- **Feature Requests**: GitHub Discussions
- **Code Contributions**: Pull Requests

## 🌈 Celebrating Success

- **Creator Spotlights** in our blog
- **Featured Stories** in our library
- **Technical Contributions** acknowledged in releases
- **Community Awards** for outstanding contributions

## 🔄 Feedback & Improvement

These guidelines evolve with our community:

- Suggest improvements via GitHub Discussions
- Participate in community surveys and feedback sessions

## 📞 Contact & Reporting

### Community Moderators
Our volunteer moderators:

- Monitor discussions for guideline adherence
- Help resolve conflicts
- Connect members with resources

### Reporting Issues

1. **Technical Issues**: Create a GitHub issue with details
2. **Community Concerns**: Contact moderators privately
3. **Urgent Safety Issues**: Email maintainers directly

---

*These guidelines reflect our commitment to curiosity, creativity, and inclusive learning. Thank you for helping make FableFlow welcoming for all storytellers.*
